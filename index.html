Get help ↗
Does sending an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. We first send you the available option, current price and conditions. Your booking is confirmed after your approval, verified payment and supplier confirmation. See our booking terms.

How long is a quoted price valid?

Airfares, rooms and package availability can change at any time. Each quote states its validity where the supplier provides one, and we recheck the price before payment.

Can I change or cancel a confirmed service?

Change and cancellation options depend on the airline, hotel, cruise line or other supplier. We will explain the applicable fee and any refundable amount before processing your request.

Does visa assistance guarantee approval?

No. Immigration authorities decide every application. We can help prepare and submit the required documents, but approval and processing time remain with the relevant authority.

When do I receive my travel documents?

We send the available ticket, voucher or application update after the service is confirmed and the required payment and documents have been received.

How can I follow up on my enquiry?

Track your enquiry with your reference, or contact the team. If you have a customer account, sign in to My Kridiya to follow your requests.
